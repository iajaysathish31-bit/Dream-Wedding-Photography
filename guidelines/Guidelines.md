# Dream Wedding Photography - Design Guidelines

## Aesthetic Stance
**Divine Luxury Light** — Modern luxury meets Indian divine elegance with a warm, welcoming light aesthetic. Ethereal, royal, nature-filled design inspired by luxury garden mandaps with golden accents, blooming lotus flowers, and abundant natural beauty. Soft cream backgrounds, golden glows, and delicate glassmorphism create a heavenly, divine, and inviting experience.

## Typography
- **Display/Headings:** Cormorant Garamond (elegant serif, refined, romantic)
- **Body:** Inter (clean, modern, highly readable)
- **Accent:** Playfair Display (for special decorative elements)

## Color Palette
- **Primary Gold:** `#D4AF37` (rich golden accent)
- **Sacred Gold:** `#F4E4C1` (soft golden glow)
- **Divine Cream:** `#FDFBF7` (warm cream background, paper-like)
- **Warm Ivory:** `#FFF9F0` (soft ivory sections)
- **Light Sand:** `#F5F1E8` (subtle section variations)
- **Lotus Pink:** `#FFE5E5` (soft blush accents)
- **Text Dark:** `#2a2a2a` (primary text color)
- **Text Muted:** `#7a7a7a` (secondary text)
- **Border Accent:** `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)` (golden borders)

## Visual Elements
- **Lotus flowers** — decorative elements, geometric patterns
- **Golden borders** — thin, elegant, with soft glow
- **Glassmorphism** — backdrop blur, subtle transparency, soft shadows
- **Mandap inspiration** — ornamental frames, sacred geometry
- **Soft glows** — golden halos, ethereal light effects
- **Sacred geometry** — 8-fold symmetry patterns

## Layout Principles
- Generous whitespace for peaceful, breathable compositions
- Asymmetric grids where appropriate for visual interest
- Full-bleed imagery with overlays
- Floating glass cards with soft shadows
- Layered depth using transparency and blur

## Motion & Interaction
- Smooth, gentle transitions (600-800ms ease-out)
- Parallax scrolling for depth
- Fade-up animations on scroll
- Hover glows on interactive elements
- Floating, gentle movements for decorative elements

## Component Patterns
- Glass cards with `backdrop-blur-xl` and golden borders
- Soft box shadows with golden tint: `0 8px 32px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.1)`
- Rounded corners (8-16px) for modern softness
- Gradient overlays on images (black to transparent)
- Button hover states with golden glow effects

## Content Voice
- Poetic, emotional, reverent
- Focus on feelings and moments over technical details
- Use real testimonials and authentic language
- Emphasize nature, light, emotion, and timelessness
